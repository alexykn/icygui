06Cluster health: is the monitoring itself healthy?

A page per environment, like Icinga Web's monitoring health. It has the zones with their endpoints (connected, last message, version, traffic), the node icygui is connected to (marked with the selected-row background), Icinga's numbers from /v1/status (checks per minute, latency, execution time, pending checks, work queues, connections, uptime), and Icinga's global switches, read-only. It opens from health in the sidebar's fixed cluster section (topic 14), from a cluster health item under the nodes in the footer switcher, and from the palette, so it stays one click away. Its data comes from the 30-second status poll icygui already makes, plus the endpoints and zones it already loads, so it adds no requests while the page is closed. The small trend lines are the last 30 minutes of those polls, kept in memory.
