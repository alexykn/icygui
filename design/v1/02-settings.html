02A settings panel in the style of Zed's

Today's two-tab modal dialog becomes a large settings panel that opens over the main window, inside it (not a window of its own): ctrl-,, the app menu or the palette open it, × or Esc close it. Approved in the review. On the left: a search field in the panel's top bar, the categories, and the sections of the open category. On the right: one row per setting, with its name, a one-line muted description, and the control right-aligned (switch, dropdown, segmented control, chips or a short field). Rows are split by the list's row rules. The rows, chips, switches and segmented controls are today's components; nothing new is invented for the controls. Changes apply at once and are written to the settings file (Zed's model). A field applies on Enter or when it loses the focus, and a bad value shows its problem under the row and isn't applied. So there is no save or cancel; edit in settings file opens settings.toml. The panel is 1080×760 in the 1440×900 window; frames 2b to 2i show the panel alone.
