13icygui on Windows: the installer, the window, the tray and toastsnew

Windows support in v1 (PLAN.md §4.2). The installer is a setup .exe built with Inno Setup 6.6 in its modern wizard, WizardStyle=modern dynamic, which follows Windows between dark and light, so every page is drawn in both. Inno fixes the layout (the header band with title and subtitle on the inner pages, Back, Next and Cancel at the bottom right), the native controls and Segoe UI. What we ship: the tall wizard image (WizardImageFile) and the small one (WizardSmallImageFile), each with its dark variant (…DynamicDark), the setup icon (SetupIconFile), the texts ([Messages], [CustomMessages]), the tasks and the uninstall question ([Tasks], [Run], [Code]). The images are made from the logo's mark in the app's palette, so the installer looks like icygui in both modes. Per-user install without admin rights is the default (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\icygui), all users as an option. The app keeps its own window and font; Windows adds its caption buttons (minimise, maximise or restore, close) at the top right of our title row, the tray icon in the notification area with its native menu, and Windows toasts. Native parts are drawn with Segoe UI (Open Sans stands in where these frames are rendered). Wall clock: Wed 7 Oct, 14:12.
