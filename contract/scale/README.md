# Scale benchmark

`generate.py` writes an Icinga 2 config with N hosts × 15 services (about 5% problems, realistic outputs, perfdata and vars). `benchmark.sh` starts a real Icinga 2.15 in Docker with it and measures load sizes and times and a forced re-check burst on the event stream. The results and the design they led to are in `docs/performance.md`.

`starts.sh [hosts] [clients]` (default 2000 and 20) starts the same Icinga in a container of its own and measures many icygui engines starting at once, first as users opening the app, then (on the restarted Icinga) as background starts with their size-proportional wait (PERF-09): when they were connected, the master's peak memory (`docker stats`) and the response time of a small by-name query that another client sends every half second (at rest and during the starts). `PHASES` chooses the phases, each on a freshly restarted Icinga: `user`, `background` (the default factors) and `background:<ms per 1 000 services>:<max s>` to compare other factors, e.g. `PHASES="user background background:1500:45" contract/scale/starts.sh 2000`. It runs `ic-core`'s ignored `starts` test, which refuses anything but that local container, and removes the container and its volume at the end. Results in `docs/performance.md` (*Quiet mode and load pacing*).
