05The host-group grid

One of the displays a view can have (topic 04; approved in the review). Every host is a 12px square, grouped by host group, coloured by its worst state: the host's own, or its worst service's. Healthy hosts are a dim green, so the problems stand out. Hollow = handled, as the list's circles: an acknowledged problem, and any host whose downtime is in effect, whatever its state (an OK host in downtime is a hollow green square). Each group's header has its worst state's dot, its name, the host count and how many hosts are in each problem state. A click on a square opens the host's pane; a click on a group filters the page to it. The grid follows the list's keyboard: arrows move a cursor over the squares, Enter opens. Squares (the default), dense enough for hundreds of hosts, or labelled cells, one per host, for small groups where the names matter: an option of the view in the dashboard editor (hosts as, 5e).
