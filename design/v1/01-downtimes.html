01Downtimes, very visible in the panes

An object in downtime says so at the top of its pane, the way Icinga Web does, instead of only through the hollow state circle. Variant A (recommended): a banner under the pane header (the app's Banner, in the Info tone), fixed above the scrolling body. It shows what is in effect, how long is left, the window, fixed or flexible, who set it and why, and remove downtime. A 2px progress line on its bottom edge shows how much of the downtime has passed. Variant B: the pane header itself becomes the downtime strip (one line, the same progress line), and the details are the first section of the body. In the banner, accent blue means in effect (the problem is handled). Grey means scheduled but not in effect yet (flexible and waiting, or in the future), so the problem is still unhandled. Wall clock in every frame: Wed 7 Oct, 14:12.
