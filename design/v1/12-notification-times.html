12Notifications: on or off per group and dashboard, and whenrevised: opt-in

Notifications are opt-in. Nothing notifies until someone turns notifications on for a group or a dashboard. Groups are off by default; a dashboard follows its group unless it is set itself, on or off. Nothing called inherit or default ever turns notifications on. Each group and each dashboard has two separate things: (1) on or off, a switch; (2) when: default, the default times and rule from the settings panel, or custom, its own times. Turning notifications on uses whichever when applies. For a dashboard, default means its group’s times: the group’s custom times if it has them, otherwise the environment’s defaults. Engineers set the times themselves; there are no fixed presets (a team’s “9 to 5” or “24/7” is just the times they set). The settings panel’s notifications page holds the defaults per environment and says that they are defaults, used by the groups and dashboards that are turned on; it lists which ones are on and which of those use custom times. A group’s notification settings (its ··· menu) and the dashboard editor’s notifications row show the switch first, then when; custom drops down the same controls as the settings page, in place. Both ··· menus get a quick notifications switch above notification settings. In the sidebar, a fixed slot shows a faint bell when a group or dashboard notifies, the clock instead when it uses custom times set on it, and nothing when it is off, so nothing moves.
