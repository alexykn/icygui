PLAN.md §4.2, Comment from the handling view. On hover (or with c on the focused thread) + comment appears at the right end of the thread’s last entry row, in its time slot: no extra row, no extra height, so nothing moves when it shows. The field opens in place as the thread’s next entry: the author (the configured name), now, and the pane’s comment field, the same component. Enter sends through the existing add-comment action, shift-Enter is a new line, Esc cancels. A sent comment shows dimmed as pending until the event stream confirms it; refused, it keeps its text with the error and retry. Without the add-comment permission it never appears. A comment belongs to the object (host or service), as in Icinga. Data: the demo’s prod-cluster; the author is n.weber. Wall clock: Thu 8 Oct, 02:20.
