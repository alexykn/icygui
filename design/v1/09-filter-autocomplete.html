09Autocomplete in the filter field, in the style of Zed

The dashboard editor's filter field (and every other filter field) completes as you type. The completions come from the parser at the cursor and from the live data: attributes with their type and a description after host. and service.; after vars., the custom variables that exist, ranked by how many objects have them; inside a string, the values in use; functions with their signatures. Matching is fuzzy, with the matched characters in the accent colour. Tab or Enter accepts, ctrl-space opens the list, Esc closes it. The popup is the app's menu card at the menu's text size, with 24px rows and 8 of them visible (a thin scroll thumb when there are more). It opens right under the line being typed, its left edge at the column where the word being completed starts, or at the field's inner left edge when it wouldn't fit there; it never sticks out of the field on the left. A documentation card of the same width explains the selected item; it always sits to the left of the list, top-aligned with it, so it never covers the field. Every info card follows that rule: the signature inside a call, hover help and an error's message sit left of the list, or left of the field when no list is open, never over or above it. Hovering a name shows the same help. A parse error is underlined where it happens. Each frame has a zoomed crop.
