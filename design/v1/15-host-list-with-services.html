15Host lists with their services

A list view that lists hosts gets a second mode, hosts as: rows | with services. rows is today’s host list, one row per host. with services draws each host as a band with its services under it, from the same parts as the service list grouped by host (10h, 10j: hostBand and hostRows in v1.js, one component): the slim band, the chevron that only folds, the counts, the paging row. The mode follows the view-controls rule: a small switch in a one-view header (a, b, e), a group in the view’s ··· when stacked (c), a row under lists in the editor (d); it is saved with the view. The only differences from 10j come from the filter picking hosts: a DOWN host always shows, a host may have no services, and a down host’s services follow hide services of hosts that are down, so its band can say 4 services hidden · host down. Data: the demo’s prod-cluster. Wall clock: Wed 7 Oct, 14:12.
