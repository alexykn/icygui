01Downtimes, very visible in the panes

An object in downtime says so at the top of its pane, the way Icinga Web does, instead of only through the hollow state circle. Variant A (chosen in the review): a banner under the pane header (the app's Banner, in the Info tone), fixed above the scrolling body. It shows what is in effect, how long is left, the window, fixed or flexible, who set it and why, and remove downtime. A 2px progress line on its bottom edge shows how much of the downtime has passed. Variant B (not chosen, kept for the record): the pane header itself becomes the downtime strip. In the banner, accent blue means in effect (the object is handled). Grey means scheduled but not in effect yet (flexible and waiting, or in the future), so a problem is still unhandled. Hollow = handled everywhere: an object whose downtime is in effect is drawn hollow in its state colour, whatever its state. In the history, downtimes take the same accent. A host downtime without all services leaves its services alone, as in Icinga Web (1j). Wall clock in every frame: Wed 7 Oct, 14:12.
