04Multi-view dashboards

A dashboard is a list of views (PLAN §2.5), stacked on one page that scrolls as a whole. Each view has a 36px header: a collapse chevron, its display's icon, its name, its filter (faint, truncated), its counts, its own sort and ···. The header uses the pane's surface, the same height as the summary bar it replaces. A view with nothing to show is only its header, with nothing to show in place of the counts. A dashboard with a single view looks exactly as it does today (header and summary bar), so rc1 dashboards don't change. Keyboard: one cursor for the whole page. j/k move through the rows and go on into the next view; Tab and shift-Tab jump to the next or previous view's first row. The view holding the cursor shows the accent bar on its header. On a view header, ←/→ collapse and expand it. ctrl-a marks every row of the focused view only.
