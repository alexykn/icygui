Round 2 after the user’s verdict on the drafts (14-comments-acks.html, round 1): two dedicated views and one pane section, built from one set of parts (threads.js). Handling (handling A): one thread per object with its acknowledgement, downtimes and free-standing comments, oldest first, like a chat. The summary bar’s kinds are filter chips: comments gives the comments view, acknowledged the acknowledged view, in the same look; there is no separate comments or acknowledged view. Downtimes: one view with two displays in the header, timeline (the default) and list. The object’s pane shows the same thread. A comment is the text typed into its acknowledge or downtime dialog, part of that entry; only free-standing comments are entries of their own. One grouping pattern everywhere: a group is the slim band of 10h (36px, the chevron at its left only collapses, a click elsewhere opens the object’s pane), entries sit under it without indent, groups page by count (7, then + N more). A host downtime with all services is one group; its services are folded by default because they are identical, and page by count when opened; a service with its own different downtime or acknowledgement is its own group.
