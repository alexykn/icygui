12Notification times: defaults, and custom times per group or dashboard

Engineers set up themselves when notifications are active. There are no fixed presets: a team’s “9 to 5” or “24/7” is just the times they set. The settings panel’s notifications page holds the defaults for an environment (today’s quiet hours, turned into the times notifications are active, with the rule that goes with them: critical and down at any time) and says that they are defaults. Every group and dashboard inherits them unless it is set to custom; the page lists, per environment, the groups and dashboards that override them, each with a link. In a group’s notification settings (its ··· menu) and in the dashboard editor’s notifications row, inherit is the default and says what it inherits; custom drops down the same controls as the settings page, in place, for that group or dashboard only. A group’s custom times pass on to its dashboards that inherit. In the sidebar, a group or dashboard with its own times shows a faint clock in a fixed slot, which stays empty otherwise, so nothing moves. Wall clock: Wed 7 Oct, 14:12.
