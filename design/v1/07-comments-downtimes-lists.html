07Lists of every comment and downtime

Two lists across all objects, from /v1/objects/comments and /v1/objects/downtimes, which icygui already loads. They open from the palette (downtimes, comments) as tabs in the sidebar's open section, and a dashboard view can list them too (frame 7e). The rows are today's list rows: the object's state circle in the mark slot, service on host, a second line with the window or the comment, and the tag. Selection, the selection bar and the keys work as in every list. Bulk removal always confirms and lists every downtime or comment it removes; acknowledgement comments and downtimes from the config are skipped, with the reason.
