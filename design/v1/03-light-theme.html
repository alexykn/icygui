03The light theme

A second Theme value with the same structure as the dark one. The window and sidebar share one surface (white); the pane is a band off it (#f7f8f9) and code blocks a band further (#f2f4f6). Rules are light greys, and the selected row is tinted towards the accent, as in dark. Text keeps the dark theme's contrast steps: muted about 5:1 and faint about 3.2:1 on its background. The state colours are the same hues, darkened to 4.3:1 or more on white: ok #2a8a4a, warning #b07408, critical #cf4646, unknown #8a5cc8, accent #2f74c0. Every token is in v1.css under .light. It is chosen in Settings → appearance (follow system, dark, light).
